01 — Overview

Project Overview

This project involved the complete redesign of a digital agency website offering services such as Social Media Marketing, Graphic Design, UI/UX Design, Web Development, Video Editing, and SEO.

The objective was to create a modern, structured, and conversion-focused experience that clearly communicates the agency's value while improving usability and engagement.

02 — Problem

Problem Statement

The existing website lacked clarity, structure, and visual consistency. Users struggled to navigate through services, understand offerings, and take action.

Poor Navigation

Unclear hierarchy made it difficult for users to find services and content.

Weak Storytelling

Visual design failed to communicate the agency's expertise and value.

Low Trust

Lack of trust-building elements reduced credibility and confidence.

Inconsistent UI

Visual inconsistency across pages broke the user experience flow.

Low Conversions

Absence of strong CTAs and clear structure hurt conversion rates.

03 — Goals

Project Goals

  • Improve overall user experience
  • Create a clean and modern UI
  • Establish strong visual hierarchy
  • Design a scalable multi-page system
  • Increase user engagement and conversions

Target Audience

  • Small to medium business owners
  • Startups and entrepreneurs
  • Personal brands
  • Companies seeking digital growth services
04 — Research

Competitive Analysis

I analyzed multiple digital agency websites to understand patterns in layout, content structure, and user engagement strategies.

  • Case studies build trust and credibility
  • Minimal design improves readability
  • Strong CTAs drive conversions
  • Structured layouts reduce confusion

Design Strategy

  • Simplicity and clarity as core principles
  • Structured content flow throughout pages
  • Strong CTA placement strategy
  • Modular and scalable design system

08 — Key Screens

Key Screens

Landing Page

The Landing Page serves as the primary entry point for the digital agency, designed to capture attention and communicate value within seconds. It addresses the user’s immediate need to understand what the agency does, who it serves, and why they should engage — driving them toward a clear next action.

Key Features / Elements

  • Hero section with a compelling headline, sub-headline, and a primary CTA button
  • Service highlights grid showcasing the six core offerings of the digital agency
  • Featured case study previews to build credibility and demonstrate results
  • Trust indicators: client logos, testimonials, or key statistics
  • Secondary CTA section encouraging users to get in touch or explore services
  • Sticky navigation for seamless access to all site sections

Design Decisions / Pillars

  • Hierarchy: Bold headline first, supporting copy second, CTA last - guides the eye naturally down the page
  • Clarity: Each section communicates a single idea, avoiding cognitive overload
  • Conversion: Above-the-fold CTA placement ensures the primary action is always visible
  • Trust: Social proof elements are strategically placed to reduce hesitation before scrolling

Services Page

The Services Page provides a structured overview of every offering available from the digital agency. It solves the user problem of not knowing the full scope of services on offer, allowing them to quickly identify which solution fits their business need and navigate deeper into individual service details.

Key Features / Elements

  • Service cards grid displaying all six core services with icons, titles, and brief descriptions
  • Individual inner pages for each service with detailed scope, deliverables, and process
  • Consistent CTA on each inner page directing users to the Contact page
  • Visual differentiation between services using iconography and color accents
  • Breadcrumb navigation to maintain context across inner service pages

Design Decisions / Pillars

  • Scannability: Card-based layout allows users to compare services at a glance without heavy reading
  • Depth on demand: Inner pages provide full detail only for users who want it, reducing overwhelm
  • Conversion: Each service page ends with a direct CTA to drive enquiries while intent is highest
  • Consistency: Shared layout template across all inner pages maintains visual cohesion

Blog Page (Main)

The Blog Main Page acts as a content hub for the digital agency, surfacing articles, guides, and industry insights. It addresses the user's need to stay informed and explore the agency's expertise, while also supporting SEO goals and organic discovery.

Key Features / Elements

  • Featured blog post hero at the top with a thumbnail, headline, and excerpt
  • Blog listing grid with article cards showing category tag, title, date, and read time
  • Category filter or tag navigation for easy content discovery
  • Search functionality to allow users to locate specific topics quickly
  • Pagination or infinite scroll for managing large content archives

Design Decisions / Pillars

  • Discoverability: Category tags and filters reduce friction in finding relevant content
  • Visual hierarchy: The featured post draws attention first, followed by the supporting grid
  • Authority: Consistent branding and quality formatting reinforces the agency's credibility as a thought leader
  • Engagement: Clear read-time indicators help users self-select articles that fit their available time

Blog Page (Inner)

The Blog Inner Page delivers the full reading experience for individual articles. It solves the user's need for focused, distraction-free content consumption while keeping them engaged with the broader site through related content and contextual CTAs.

Key Features / Elements

  • Article hero with a cover image, title, author name, date, and category tag
  • Readable body content with proper typographic hierarchy using headings and paragraph spacing
  • Sticky table of contents sidebar (desktop) for long-form navigation
  • Related articles section at the bottom to encourage continued reading
  • CTA block within or at the end of the article to capture interested readers

Design Decisions / Pillars

  • Readability: Optimised line length, font size, and spacing ensure a comfortable reading experience
  • Retention: Related articles reduce exit rates by surfacing relevant next reads
  • Conversion: Inline CTA blocks capture leads when user interest is at its peak
  • Clarity: Minimal distractions keep focus on the content itself

Case Study Page (Main)

The Case Study Main Page aggregates all completed work for the digital agency into a browsable portfolio. It directly addresses the prospective client's need to evaluate past performance, industry experience, and the calibre of results delivered before making a contact decision.

Key Features / Elements

  • Case study cards with project thumbnail, client industry, service type, and headline result
  • Filter tabs by service category to help users find relevant work quickly
  • Outcome metrics displayed prominently on each card to communicate impact at a glance
  • Clean grid layout that scales to accommodate a growing portfolio
  • Page-level CTA encouraging users to discuss their own project

Design Decisions / Pillars

  • Trust: Displaying real results up front on cards reduces the need for users to dig for proof
  • Relevance: Filtering by service type ensures users see the work most applicable to their needs
  • Scalability: Grid-based layout allows new case studies to be added without restructuring the page
  • Conversion: Strong page-level CTA capitalises on the trust built by browsing the portfolio

Case Study Page (Inner)

Each Case Study Inner Page tells the complete story of a single project from problem to process to measurable outcome. It serves users who want to thoroughly evaluate how the digital agency approaches challenges before committing to an enquiry.

Key Features / Elements

  • Project hero with overview stats: industry, service type, timeline, and key result
  • Structured narrative flow: Problem to Research to Solution to Results
  • Visual showcases of deliverables: mockups, screenshots, or before/after comparisons
  • Quantified outcome metrics displayed in a highlight card or stat grid
  • Navigation to previous/next case studies and a closing CTA

Design Decisions / Pillars

  • Storytelling: A linear narrative structure mirrors how readers naturally process information: context, journey, resolution
  • Credibility: Real data and visual deliverables validate claims more effectively than descriptive text alone
  • Usability: Clear section headers allow readers to skim to the parts most relevant to their evaluation
  • Conversion: A closing CTA placed after results is positioned at peak persuasion

Contact Us Page

The Contact Us Page is the final conversion touchpoint, bridging the user's interest and the agency's first response. It solves the friction of reaching out by making the process intuitive, low-effort, and reassuring, ensuring qualified leads are not lost at the final step.

Key Features / Elements

  • Short, focused contact form: name, email, service of interest, and message fields
  • Clear headline and supporting copy to reassure users before they submit
  • Contact details section: email address, phone number (if applicable), and social links
  • Response time expectation to build confidence and reduce post-submission anxiety
  • Optional: embedded map or location reference if the agency has a physical presence

Design Decisions / Pillars

  • Simplicity: A minimal form with only essential fields reduces submission friction and increases completion rates
  • Trust: Setting a clear response expectation removes uncertainty and encourages users to follow through
  • Accessibility: Proper label alignment, input sizing, and error states ensure the form works for all users
  • Conversion focus: No competing links or distractions on the page; the single goal is form submission

10 — Challenges
Challenges
  • Managing multiple pages without clutter
  • Maintaining visual consistency throughout
  • Balancing aesthetics and usability
  • Structuring large amounts of content
Solutions
  • Modular design system with reusable patterns
  • Clear visual hierarchy across all pages
  • Reusable components for consistency
  • Consistent spacing and grid usage
11 — Outcomes

Improved Usability

Clearer navigation and structured layout made it easier for users to find what they need.

Better Engagement

Compelling visual storytelling increased time on site and reduced drop-offs.

Increased Trust

Case studies and consistent UI built credibility with potential clients.

Higher Conversions

Strategic CTA placement and clear value communication improved conversion rate.

12 — Learnings

Clarity Over Complexity

Simpler, clearer design always outperforms complex aesthetics for service-based sites.

Structure Improves UX

A well-defined information architecture reduces user confusion and improves satisfaction.

Case Studies Build Trust

Showing real work and process is one of the strongest trust signals for agencies.

Consistency Builds Professionalism

A design system ensures every page feels intentional and cohesive across the entire site.
